About the maker

Inna Andrejeva

Founder of with_love_by_inna

I create knitwear where aesthetics meet quiet and inner comfort — pieces that bring a sense of calm confidence and gentle strength.

At the heart of it are natural Italian yarns — cashmere, merino, mohair with silk, linen — and their subtle combinations. Every piece is hand-finished in every detail, from silhouette to how it feels against the skin.

It is timeless — not tied to trends or seasons, with a value that lasts for years.

For women who choose quality, awareness, and quiet luxury.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a piece you like and message me on WhatsApp or Instagram. Size, colour, timing and order details are agreed individually.

Can I choose another size or colour?

Yes. Size is selected individually, and the colours shown are examples. Other shades can be discussed when ordering.

How long does an order take?

Each piece is made to order. The production time depends on the model and current workload and is confirmed before work begins.

How does delivery work?

Worldwide shipping is available from Latvia. Shipping cost depends on the destination and parcel size. Full details are available in Orders & Policies.
